# RollingCam Support

**Contact:** open an issue on this site's repository or email the address listed on the App Store page.

## Frequently asked questions

**Where do my videos go?**
Into the Photos app, in your library and the Recents album. RollingCam keeps nothing else.

**Why does recording stop when I lock the screen or switch apps?**
iOS does not allow apps to use the camera in the background. When RollingCam leaves the foreground it saves the current buffer and stops.

**The app was closed unexpectedly. Is my video lost?**
No. The buffer is written to disk as you record. On the next launch RollingCam recovers it and saves it to Photos.

**How long is the saved clip?**
The buffer window you chose in Settings, plus up to five extra seconds.

**What is the privacy screen?**
An optional black or clock cover shown while recording. Double-tap to reveal the controls.
