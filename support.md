# RollingCam Support

**Contact:** open an issue on this site's repository or email the address listed on the App Store page.

## Frequently asked questions

**Where do my recordings go?**
Video clips go to the Photos app, in your library and the Recents album. Audio clips go to the Files app, under On My iPhone > RollingCam. Both also appear in the Recordings list inside the app (the list button at the bottom left).

**How long is the saved clip?**
The time you chose under Keep last in Settings, plus up to five extra seconds. You can choose from 30 seconds up to 24 hours. Settings shows how much free space a clip of that length needs.

**Why does video recording stop when I lock the screen or switch apps?**
iOS does not allow apps to use the camera in the background. When RollingCam leaves the foreground it saves the current buffer and stops.

**Can I keep recording with the iPhone locked?**
Yes, in audio-only mode. Choose Audio only under Input in Settings. Recording continues in the background and while the iPhone is locked, until you stop it.

**How do I record the back and front cameras together?**
Choose Front + back cameras under Input in Settings. One camera fills the picture and the other appears in a small window. Tap the small window to swap them, or drag it to another corner. This mode records at 1080p 30 fps and is offered only on iPhones that can run both cameras at once.

**How do I zoom?**
Pinch the preview. The back camera switches lenses automatically. Tap the zoom button to go back to 1×.

**Why does the app ask for access to my photo library?**
Saving a clip needs permission to add to Photos. Playing or deleting a video clip from the Recordings list needs permission to read your library, and iOS asks the first time you do it. If you decline, your clips are still saved and you can open them in the Photos app.

**I deleted a video from the Recordings list by mistake. Can I get it back?**
Yes. It goes to Recently Deleted in the Photos app, where you can recover it for about 30 days. Deleted audio clips cannot be recovered.

**The app was closed unexpectedly. Is my recording lost?**
No. The buffer is written to disk as you record. On the next launch RollingCam recovers it and saves it.

**What is the privacy screen?**
An optional black or clock cover shown while recording. Double-tap to reveal the controls.
