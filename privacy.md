# RollingCam Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 7, 2026_

RollingCam is designed so that your data never leaves your device.

## What the app accesses

- **Camera and microphone** to record video and audio while the app is open and you have started recording.
- **Photo library (add only)** to save the clips you choose to keep.

## What the app collects

Nothing. RollingCam has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting of its own, and makes no network connections.

## Where your data is stored

Video is written temporarily to the app's private storage on your device while recording, then saved to your Photos library. Temporary files are deleted after each save. Nothing is uploaded or shared with the developer or any third party.

## Children

RollingCam does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.
