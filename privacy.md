# RollingCam Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 10, 2026_

RollingCam does not send your data anywhere. Everything it records stays on your iPhone, in places you control.

## What the app accesses

- **Camera and microphone**, to record video and audio after you start a recording. Video recording runs only while the app is open. In audio-only mode, recording continues while the app is in the background or the iPhone is locked, until you stop it. iOS shows its microphone indicator the whole time.
- **Photo library, adding**, to save your video clips to Photos.
- **Photo library, reading and deleting**, only when you play or delete a video clip from the app's Recordings list. iOS asks for your permission the first time. RollingCam looks up only the clips it saved itself and does not look through the rest of your library. If you decline, clips are still saved, and you can open them in the Photos app.

## What the app collects

Nothing. RollingCam has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting of its own, and it makes no network connections. The Privacy Policy and Support links in Settings open this website in your browser.

## Where your data is stored

- **While recording**, video and audio are written to temporary files in the app's private storage. They are deleted after each save.
- **Video clips** are saved to your Photos library.
- **Audio clips** are saved to the RollingCam folder in the Files app and stay there until you delete them.
- **A list of your saved video clips** (date, length, size and the clip's identifier in Photos) is kept in the app's private storage, so the Recordings list can show them.

Nothing is uploaded to or shared with the developer or any third party. If you use iCloud Photos or iCloud Backup, Apple may store your Photos library and the app's files under your own Apple account, according to your iCloud settings.

## Deleting your data

Delete a clip from the Recordings list, from Photos, or from the Files app. Video clips deleted from the Recordings list go to Recently Deleted in Photos. Deleting the app removes its audio clips, its list of saved clips and any temporary files. Video clips already in Photos stay there.

## Children

RollingCam does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
