# RollingCam

A rewind recorder for iPhone. Tap record once and leave it running. RollingCam holds only the most recent window of video or audio (30 seconds to 24 hours) and saves just that when you stop.

- Record with one camera, with the back and front cameras together, or with the microphone only.
- Video clips are saved to your Photos. Audio clips are saved to the RollingCam folder in the Files app.
